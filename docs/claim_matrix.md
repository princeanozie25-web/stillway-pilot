## Strongest prior-art challenge

Koul, Greydanus and Fern (2019), extended by Carr et al. (2020), plus triviality by construction. They already quantise recurrent memory, extract a machine, minimise it and verify it. In our world the codes are the only channel between steps, so any correct planner must hold the obligation in the codes and enumeration must end. A lookup table with the same number of states would pass the basic tests. The pilot must therefore show something that a continuous planner quantised after training does not give. We ran that comparison. See `REPORT.md`.

## Not verified

- The PMLR v306 page numbers for Li et al.
- Whether the guarantees in Carr et al. and WAE-MDP are exact or approximate (abstracts only).
- Mini et al. 2023 was read from a search summary, not the page.
- No dedicated search was run for Ferns-style bisimulation metrics.
